I am Sihan Qin, currently a fourth-year undergraduate majoring in Computer Science and Technology at Sun Yat-sen University.

I previously worked as a research intern at HCP Lab and ISEE Lab at Sun Yat-sen University, focusing on physical world modeling, robotic manipulation, and post-training with reinforcement learning.

I am currently an Embodied AI Algorithm Intern at BrainCo. I lead a semi-automated prosthetics project, exploring how to combine robot control policies with human intent.

Zotero Paper Partner answers questions without disrupting your focus. Once you write a question in your notes, you can keep reading; the plugin works quietly in the background and adds the answer below the question. My aim is to extend our ability to understand while preserving our attention.

Semi-automated prosthetics · BrainCo

I am working toward a semi-automated prosthesis in which a person and a dexterous hand work together. The person uses visual and tactile feedback to plan tasks and high-level actions. Human intent is conveyed to the robotic system through EMG, and the robot’s control policy carries out dexterous manipulation guided by that intent.
