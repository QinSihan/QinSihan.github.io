HCP Lab, Sun Yat-sen University

Jul 2025 — Mar 2026

Research Intern · Advised by Prof. Guangrun Wang and Prof. Liang Lin · Guangzhou, China

World models and robotic manipulation

This research explored how knowledge of dynamics learned through video pretraining could support robotic manipulation. I worked on the Physical Autoregressive Model (PAR), which jointly predicts future observations and robot actions and uses new observations after execution to inform subsequent predictions.

My work

  • Contributed to the formulation and development of the model, exploring joint modeling of visual observations and robot actions.
  • Trained and evaluated models in ManiSkill, LIBERO, and CALVIN, tuning batch size, learning rate, and learning-rate schedules.
  • Conducted experiments on a Franka Panda arm and collected 80 teleoperated demonstration trajectories for supervised learning.
  • Contributed to paper writing.
